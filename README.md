# Magma Grill Digital Fire

Build a complete, premium, dark 'all-black' themed landing page and digital menu web app for Magma Grill (Presidente Prudente - SP).

Style & Aesthetic:
- Dark luxury steakhouse / BBQ & burger vibe (deep black #0b0b0b, dark charcoal cards, warm ember/lava orange #ff6b35 and amber accents, elegant typography, glow effects).
- Same high-impact, modern all-black aesthetic as top artisanal burger & barbecue places.

Brand details provided by user:
- Name: Magma Grill - Churrasco, Almoço, Jantar e Delivery Expresso
- City: Presidente Prudente - SP
- Address: R. João Pedro Pereira, 840 - Presidente Prudente - SP, 19064-170
- WhatsApp: (18) 99758-1954 (https://wa.me/5518997581954)
- Instagram: @bbqmagma (https://www.instagram.com/bbqmagma/)
- Google Maps: https://maps.app.goo.gl/C4U1iYLRannrtDhz5
- Hours: Almoço (segunda a sexta das 11h às 14h30), Jantar (segunda a sábado das 18h às 23h)

Structure & Sections (long, full-featured page):
1. Sticky Header / Navbar with Magma Grill logo, navigation links, and direct WhatsApp CTA.
2. Impactful Hero Section: headline 'A Força do Fogo, o Ponto Perfeito', badges ('Cortes Nobres na Brasa', 'Almoço & Jantar', 'Delivery Rápido'), CTAs for WhatsApp order and menu anchor.
3. Feature Badges bar: 'Carne 100% Selecionada', 'Brasa de Verdade', 'Embalagem Térmica', 'Entrega Expressa'.
4. Interactive Digital Menu with WhatsApp Cart:
   - Category filter tabs: Cortes na Brasa, Hambúrgueres Artesanais, Pratos Executivos (Almoço), Porções & Petiscos (Batata Rústica, Queijo Coalho com Melaço, Pão de Alho Especial), Bebidas e Sobremesas.
   - Products with realistic mock items, mouth-watering descriptions, prices in R$ (BRL), and 'Adicionar ao Pedido' buttons.
   - Sticky Cart Drawer / Floating Bar with live total calculation, order summary, customer name/address input, and one-click 'Enviar Pedido no WhatsApp' formatted text generator.
5. Sobre a Magma / O Segredo da Brasa: section highlighting technique, quality, smoke, and passion for good food.
6. Galeria de Fotos / Experiência: visual showcase of grill, cuts, burgers, and atmosphere.
7. Depoimentos / Avaliações: 5-star customer reviews from Presidente Prudente.
8. Status de Funcionamento: badge indicating current open status / next service.
9. Localização & Horários: Map preview, address, copy address button, direct Google Maps link.
10. Footer completo com links úteis, redes sociais e canal direto de atendimento.
